FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much is the buffet?

Buffet prices change by day and time. Call (843) 839-4044 or stop by for today's price. Coupons: $2 off anytime or $5 off before 4 PM on the adult buffet.

Do you have all-you-can-eat king crab legs?

Yes. Crabby George's is the only restaurant in Myrtle Beach with all-you-can-eat Alaskan king crab legs. Add it to your adult buffet for an additional price and your server brings king crab legs to your table from the kitchen, as many rounds as you want. You can also order a single 1 lb plate from the menu for $79.99.

Are crab legs on the buffet?

Yes. Alaskan snow crab legs are on the buffet line at no extra charge. All-you-can-eat Alaskan king crab legs are an add-on served by your server, and the 22 oz whole Maine lobster is ordered from the menu.

Can I order without the buffet?

Yes. Seafood and steak entrées come with 2 sides, and you can order online for pickup at order.crabbygeorges.com.

Do you take reservations?

Yes. Book online at crabbygeorges.com/reservations or call (843) 839-4044.

Is there a kids menu?

Yes. Kids meals for ages 5 and under include chicken tenders, corn dogs, fried rice and corn on the cob.

What are your hours?

Monday to Thursday 3:30 PM to 9 PM, Friday 2 PM to 9:30 PM, Saturday 12 PM to 10 PM and Sunday 12 PM to 9:30 PM. Holiday hours may differ.

Where are you located?

7904 N Kings Hwy, Myrtle Beach, SC 29572, a few blocks from the oceanfront and minutes from Broadway at the Beach.

What is Calabash style seafood?

Calabash style means seafood that is lightly breaded and quickly fried, a tradition from the Carolina coast. Our fried bar has Calabash style shrimp, oysters, scallops, flounder and more.

How many items are on the buffet?

More than 120 items, including snow crab legs, raw oysters, shrimp, mussels, clams, prime rib, steaks, fried seafood, Southern sides and desserts.

Do you have outdoor seating?

Yes, we have a patio for outdoor dining.

Can I use more than one coupon?

No. Coupons are for the adult buffet only, cannot be combined with other discounts, and must be shown when you arrive.
