FAQ

Good to know

How much is the buffet?

Buffet prices change by day and time. Call (843) 839-4044 or stop by for today's price. Coupons: $2 off anytime or $5 off before 4 PM on the adult buffet.

Are crab legs on the buffet?

Yes. Alaskan snow crab legs are on the buffet. Alaskan king crab legs (1 lb) and the 22 oz whole Maine lobster are ordered separately from the menu.

Can I order without the buffet?

Yes. Seafood and steak entrées come with 2 sides, and you can order online for pickup at order.crabbygeorges.com.

Do you take reservations?

Yes. Book online at reserve.crabbygeorges.com or call (843) 839-4044.

Is there a kids menu?

Yes. Kids meals for ages 5 and under include chicken tenders, corn dogs, fried rice and corn on the cob.
